About Our School

Building Futures Through Quality Education Since 1990

Our History

Founded in 1990, ABC Higher Secondary School has been a beacon of educational excellence for over three decades. What began as a small institution with just 100 students has now grown into one of the most prestigious educational establishments in the region, serving over 850 students annually.

Throughout our journey, we have remained committed to our founding principles of academic excellence, character development, and holistic education. Our state-of-the-art facilities, experienced faculty, and innovative teaching methods have consistently produced outstanding results, with our students excelling not just in academics but also in sports, arts, and other extracurricular activities.

Today, ABC Higher Secondary School stands as a testament to the power of dedicated education, having shaped the futures of thousands of students who have gone on to become successful professionals, leaders, and responsible citizens contributing to society.

Our Vision & Mission

Guiding principles that drive our commitment to excellence

Our Vision

To be a globally recognized institution that nurtures future leaders through excellence in education, innovation, and character development. We envision creating a learning environment where every student discovers their potential and develops the skills, values, and knowledge necessary to thrive in an ever-changing world.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, comprehensive education that empowers students to achieve academic excellence, develop critical thinking skills, and become responsible global citizens. We are committed to fostering a supportive, inclusive environment that encourages creativity, innovation, and lifelong learning while instilling strong moral and ethical values.

Our Core Values

The principles that define who we are and how we serve our students

Excellence

We strive for the highest standards in everything we do, from academics to character development.

Integrity

We promote honesty, transparency, and ethical behavior in all aspects of school life.

Innovation

We embrace new ideas and modern teaching methods to enhance the learning experience.

Community

We foster a supportive, inclusive environment where everyone feels valued and respected.

Responsibility

We encourage students to take ownership of their learning and become responsible citizens.

Compassion

We cultivate empathy and kindness towards others in our diverse community.
